About Sona Impex

A Legacy of Quality Filtration & Manufacturing Excellence

Delivering Reliable Industrial Filtration Solutions

Established with a commitment to quality and reliability, Sona Impex has grown into a trusted manufacturer and supplier of industrial filtration products. With decades of experience, we provide high-performance filter bags, filter cloth, filter cartridges, and customized filtration solutions designed for demanding industrial applications.

Our products are widely used in pharmaceutical, chemical, food processing, cement, petrochemical, steel, and other manufacturing industries where process efficiency, product purity, and operational reliability are critical.

By combining manufacturing expertise, stringent quality control, and customer-focused service, we strive to deliver filtration solutions that consistently meet industry requirements and build long-term business relationships worldwide.
